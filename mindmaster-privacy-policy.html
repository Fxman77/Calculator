🧠 MindMaster — Cognitive Brain Training

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Overview & Commitment

MindMaster ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a mobile cognitive brain training game designed to help users enhance memory, reaction speed, mental arithmetic, focus, and logic. We hold user privacy to the highest standard.

✓ Zero Personal Data Collection: MindMaster does NOT require account creation, registration, login credentials, or any personally identifiable information (such as your full name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts list, or precise GPS location).

2. Offline-First Local Data Storage

MindMaster is built with an offline-first architecture. All user game progression and gameplay statistics are stored strictly locally on your device (within protected app-private storage):

  • Progress through the 100 Cognitive Challenge Levels
  • Earned Stars (⭐, ⭐⭐, ⭐⭐⭐) and High Scores per level
  • Cognitive Domain Ratings (Memory, Speed, Logic, Focus, Math)
  • Daily Workout Streak (🔥) and completion timestamps
  • Local user settings (sound effects, haptic feedback, display preferences)

This gameplay data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or external database.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and offer MindMaster as a free educational game, we integrate third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and technical device information, strictly in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Anonymous Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • General coarse location (country or region-level network IP)
  • Aggregated ad performance, impression, and interaction metrics

For detailed information on how Google manages and protects advertising data, please review: Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

4. European User Consent (GDPR & UK TCF v2.2)

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, MindMaster fully complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2).

Upon initial launch, users in applicable jurisdictions are presented with a consent dialogue via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • Consent for Personalized Ads: Advertising partners may use anonymized identifiers to display relevant ads.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Only contextual advertisements that do not rely on personalized tracking are delivered.

You can revoke or modify your advertising consent preferences at any time directly through the in-game Settings menu.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

MindMaster is designed to provide safe, wholesome cognitive entertainment for general audiences and families. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union), in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Developer Families Policies.

6. Data Retention & User Control (Data Deletion)

Because MindMaster does not collect or maintain remote user accounts, we do not store any personal records on external cloud servers. You retain complete, autonomous control over all local game data:

  • Clear App Data: You can permanently erase all local game data at any time via Android Device Settings: Settings > Apps > MindMaster > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Removing the app from your device automatically and irreversibly deletes all locally cached progress and preference files.
